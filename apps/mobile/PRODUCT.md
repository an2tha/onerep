# OneRep
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
OneRep brings workout logging, nutrition, progress, and optional AI coaching into one app. The mobile web app also runs inside a Capacitor wrapper.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Repository evidence: workout routines and scheduling, nutrition and recipes, hydration, body measurements, health integrations, and Coach. Onboarding must explain these capabilities, expose configuration including light/dark mode, preserve safety and consent, and preview imports before committing them.

## Product Principles
The user requested a complete onboarding redesign and delegated visual judgment. Use structured setup, visible navigation, feature previews, and an editable final review. Core tracking works without AI.
